Privacy Policy

How PhysBox handles data across physbox.io and the Volt, Mesh, Etch, and Flux engineering simulators.

Local-First Execution

Core simulations (SPICE, MuJoCo, OpenSCAD, and CAM toolpaths) execute in WebAssembly directly in your browser without requiring an account.

Zero Ad Trackers

PhysBox uses no third-party advertising networks, cross-site tracking pixels, or data brokers. We never sell user data or engineering designs.

Optional Cloud Sync

Designs, presets, and machine telemetry only leave your browser when you sign in to sync them or explicitly generate a shareable link.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to physbox.io, its browser-based engineering applications (volt.physbox.io, mesh.physbox.io, etch.physbox.io, and flux.physbox.io), the PhysBox cloud API (api.physbox.io), and the PhysBox Native desktop builds (collectively, the "Services").

2. Local Browser Simulation & Hardware Control

By default, you can use the PhysBox simulation and fabrication tools without signing in:

  • In-Browser Computation: Circuit netlists, 3D CAD geometry, physics scenes, STL/3MF meshes, SVG layouts, and CNC/laser G-code are computed locally on your device via WebAssembly and WebGL.
  • Local Storage: Unsynced presets, workspace preferences, and draft scenes are saved in your browser's local storage on your own machine.
  • WebSerial Hardware-in-the-Loop: Direct USB connections to microcontrollers, GRBL CNC routers, and laser cutters use the browser's WebSerial API locally between your computer and your hardware.

3. Information We Collect When You Choose to Interact

We collect personal or account-linked information only when you actively sign in, use cloud features, or contact us:

  • Account Sign-In (Google OAuth 2.0): When you sign in via Google Identity Services, we receive your verified email address, display name, and profile picture URL to create and authenticate your PhysBox account. PhysBox never receives or stores your Google password.
  • Cloud Saves & Remote Machine Telemetry (PhysBox Pro): When signed in, saved documents/scenes, cross-app UI preferences, fabrication job run logs, and paired remote controller identifiers (such as Tekno Box device IDs) are stored on your account so you can access them across devices.
  • Optional AI Copilot API Keys: If you use the in-app AI Copilot, prompts and scene context are sent directly to the model provider you select (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) using the API key you provide. If you save an API key in Account Settings so it follows your account across apps, it is transmitted over TLS, stored encrypted on the server, and can be deleted at any time.
  • Contact Forms & Product Updates: When you submit a message on Contact, join the Early Access list, or subscribe to release updates, we store the name, email address, and message you provide solely to respond or send the updates you requested.
  • Standard Server Logs: Our hosting infrastructure (Google Cloud Run) records standard operational request metadata (such as IP address, HTTP status, user-agent, and timestamp) for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

4. Cookies & First-Party Storage

PhysBox uses strictly necessary and functional first-party cookies scoped to physbox.io (with SameSite=Lax and Secure over HTTPS) so a single sign-in and consistent preferences work across physbox.io, volt.physbox.io, mesh.physbox.io, and etch.physbox.io:

  • physbox_session & physbox_session_shared: Maintains your authenticated session across the PhysBox suite.
  • physbox_dark_mode, physbox_touch_plate_mm, physbox_shim_mm, physbox_machine_link, physbox_cloud_device_id: Remembers display theme and machine setup parameters across apps.

We do not set third-party advertising, analytics profiling, or retargeting cookies.

5. How Information Is Shared & Secured

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or engineering files. Data is processed only by essential infrastructure providers acting on our behalf (such as Google Cloud Platform for hosting/storage and Google Identity Services for authentication), or when required by applicable law. All network traffic between your browser and *.physbox.io is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

6. Your Choices, Retention & Deletion

  • You can sign out at any time from the account menu to clear your session cookie and local profile cache.
  • You can remove saved AI API keys or revoke personal API tokens directly in Account Settings, and delete cloud-saved scenes inside each app.
  • To request an export or complete deletion of your PhysBox account and associated cloud records, contact us via physbox.io/contact.html.

7. Contact

Questions or requests regarding privacy and security can be submitted at physbox.io/contact.html.
